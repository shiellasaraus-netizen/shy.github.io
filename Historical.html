Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo

Is a 2016 historical fantasy drama where 25-year-old 21st-century woman Go Ha-jin (IU) travels back in time to the Goryeo Dynasty during a solar eclipse. Trapped in the body of Hae Soo, she becomes entangled in romantic relationships and treacherous power struggles among the ruling Wang family princes.

Alchemy of Souls: Light and Shadow (Season 2)

3 years later, in the country of Daeho, Jang Uk (Lee Jae-Wook) spends his lonely days hunting soul shifters. He carries the ice stone within him that allowed him to be reborn. In Jinyowon, Jin Bu-Yeon (Go Youn-Jung) has lost all of her memories and divine powers. She was pulled out of Lake Gyeongcheondaeho and brought back to life with the help of Master Lee Cheol (Lim Chul-Soo). Jin Bu-Yeon is now kept hidden in a secret room within Jinyowon.

Dear Hongrang

A Joseon-era merchant family searching for their lost heir, Hong-rang, who returns 12 years later as a mysterious stranger. His half-sister, Jae-yi (Jo Bo-ah), investigates the man (Lee Jae-wook) claiming to be her brother, uncovering dark secrets.

Faith

Is a 2012 Korean historical-fantasy drama starring Lee Min-ho and Kim Hee-sun. The story follows a modern-day plastic surgeon kidnapped by a Goryeo-era warrior to heal a queen, resulting in a love that spans 700 years amidst political turmoil.

Hwarang

Is a South Korean historical drama about an elite group of young male warriors in the Silla Kingdom (57 BC–AD 935). Amidst political turmoil, Queen Jiso creates the "Hwarang" to protect the young, hidden King Jinheung (Park Hyung-sik) and train them to be loyal fighters, while the young warriors discover friendship, love, and their own identities.

The King in Love

Is a Goryeo-set romantic melodrama following Wang Won (Im Si-wan), a charming but ambitious crown prince, his loyal bodyguard Wang Rin (Hong Jong-hyun), and Eun San (Im Yoon-ah), a wealthy woman who captures both their hearts. The trio's deep friendship fractures into a tragic love triangle, testing loyalty against love.

The Moon Embracing the Sun

Is a hit 20-episode Korean historical-fantasy drama starring Kim Soo-hyun and Han Ga-in. It tells a romantic, political tale of a fictional Joseon King, Lee Hwon, and a shaman named Wol (originally noblewoman Yeon-woo), who was assumed dead due to political conspiracies but returns to court to reclaim her place.

Empress Ki

Is an epic historical drama (sageuk) that follows the survival and rise of Ki Seung-nyang, a Goryeo-born woman who eventually becomes a powerful empress of the Yuan Dynasty. The 51-episode series is a fictionalized account of the historical Empress Ki and is known for its intense political intrigue and tragic love triangle.

100 Days my Prince

Is a 16-episode romantic comedy-drama about Lee Yul, a cold Crown Prince who loses his memory after an assassination attempt and is forced to live as a commoner named Won-deuk. He marries Hong-shim, a strong-willed woman running a detective agency, and the couple navigates marriage and political intrigue for 100 days.

Queen for Seven Days

Is a 20-episode historical melodrama about Lady Shin (Park Min-young), who becomes Queen Dangyeong but is dethroned after only seven days due to political conspiracies. It follows her tragic, passionate romance with Grand Prince Lee Yeok (Yeon Woo-jin) while caught between him and his jealous, tyrannical brother, King Yeonsangun (Lee Dong-gun).

The Red Sleeve

Is a highly acclaimed historical romance drama depicting the, often tragic, love story between King Jeongjo (Lee Jun-ho) and royal court lady Seong Deok-im (Lee Se-young). Based on a novel, it highlights the conflict between personal love and duty, as Deok-im seeks to protect her independence while the King prioritizes his nation.

Under the Queen’s Umbrella

Is a 16-episode historical drama (sageuk) following Queen Im Hwa-ryeong (Kim Hye-soo) as she struggles to transform her mischievous "troublemaker" sons into worthy crown princes. Amidst cutthroat palace politics and intense competition for the throne, the Queen fights to protect her children from the Queen Dowager and hostile consorts.

The Tale of Nokdu

Is a Joseon-era romantic comedy-thriller about Jeon Nok-du (Jang Dong-yoon), a man who disguises himself as a woman to infiltrate a mysterious women-only village, and Dong Dong-ju (Kim So-hyun), a fiery, revenge-driven gisaeng-in-training. Nok-du uncovers secrets about his birth and falls in love while navigating political intrigue.

The Haunted Palace

Is a 16-episode historical fantasy rom-com focusing on Yeo-ri (Kim Ji-yeon), a shaman who rejects her fate to work as a spectacle artisan. Her life shatters when her first love, Royal Scribe Yoon Gap (Yook Sung-jae), is possessed by an ancient, grumpy serpent spirit named Kang Chul, forcing them together.

Mr. Sunshine

Is a 2018 historical melodrama that follows Eugene Choi, a man born into slavery in Joseon who escapes to the United States during the 1871 Shinmiyangyo incident. Decades later, he returns to his homeland as a U.S. Marine Corps officer on a mission. While in Korea, he falls in love with Go Ae-shin, a noblewoman who is secretly a highly skilled sniper for the Righteous Army fighting for Korea's independence. Their romance is complicated by the rigid caste system, foreign political conspiracies, and a shared commitment to a nation on the brink of Japanese colonization.

Kingdom

Is a historical political thriller and horror series set in Korea's medieval Joseon period. The story follows Crown Prince Lee Chang (Ju Ji-hoon) as he investigates a mysterious illness affecting his father, the King. He soon discovers that the King has become a flesh-eating monster—the result of a failed resurrection attempt—and that a deadly plague is rapidly spreading across the land, turning the dead into "monsters".

Bon Appetit, Your Majesty

Is a Netflix romantic comedy/fantasy drama about Yeon Ji-yeong, a celebrated modern-day French chef who travels back to the Joseon Dynasty during a solar eclipse. She becomes the personal chef for the ruthless, trauma-burdened King Lee Heon, using modern culinary techniques to win his heart and survive palace politics.

The Kings Affection

Is a historical romance K-drama about Dam-i, a woman forced to disguise herself as her deceased twin brother, taking his place as the Joseon Crown Prince to avoid execution. She navigates dangerous palace politics and a romantic entanglement with her royal tutor, Jung Ji-woon.

Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung

Is a 20-episode Korean historical romance (available on Netflix) starring Shin Se-kyung and Cha Eun-woo. Set in the 19th-century Joseon Dynasty, it follows a bold, intellectual woman, Goo Hae-ryung, who challenges patriarchal norms to become one of the first female palace historians while falling in love with a hidden, romance-novel-writing prince.

Love in the Moonlight

Is a Joseon-era historical romantic comedy about Hong Ra-on (Kim Yoo-jung), a woman disguised as a man giving dating advice, who accidentally becomes a eunuch to the witty Crown Prince Lee Yeong (Park Bo-gum). It follows their forbidden romance, political intrigue, and personal growth.
