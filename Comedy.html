Welcome to Waikiki

Welcome to Waikiki tells the story of three young men struggling to run a failing guesthouse called Waikiki while chasing their own dreams. Their already messy lives become even more chaotic when a single mother and her baby unexpectedly move into the guesthouse. Filled with hilarious misunderstandings, friendship, and heartfelt moments, the series focuses on youth, ambition, and surviving adulthood.

Twinkling Watermelon

Twinkling Watermelon follows Eun-gyeol, a musically gifted teenager who is the only hearing member of a deaf family. After mysteriously traveling back to the past, he meets his young parents before they fell in love and joins a band with his father. The story blends music, family bonds, friendship, and time travel as he tries to change their future while discovering his own dreams.

Weightlifting Fairy Kim Bok Joo

Strong Woman Do Bong Soon follows Do Bong-soon, a petite young woman born with extraordinary superhuman strength passed down through generations of women in her family. She is hired as a bodyguard by a playful game company CEO, Ahn Min-hyuk, while also dealing with a dangerous kidnapping case in her neighborhood. The drama mixes action, comedy, romance, and female empowerment as Bong-soon learns to use her powers for justice and love.

Strong woman Do Bong-soon

is a romantic comedy-thriller about Do Bong-soon (Park Bo-young), a woman born with hereditary superhuman strength. She becomes the bodyguard for Ahn Min-hyuk (Park Hyung-sik), the quirky CEO of a gaming company, while trying to catch a serial kidnapper in her neighborhood, balancing action with romance.

Sold Out on You

is a South Korean romantic comedy on Netflix following an unlikely romance between a workaholic, insomniac home shopping host (Chae Won-bin) and a secretive, perfectionist farmer/natural cosmetics CEO (Ahn Hyo-seop). As they collaborate, they heal their past traumas, with Kim Bum co-starring as a corporate executive involved in a love triangle.

Fight for my Way

is a romantic comedy-drama following four underachieving childhood friends—the "Fantastic Four"—struggling to achieve their dreams despite lacking top-tier qualifications. The story focuses on Ko Dong-man, a former taekwondo star turned UFC fighter, and Choi Ae-ra, a department store receptionist striving to be a TV announcer.

Oh my Venus

is a 16-episode romantic comedy about Kang Joo-eun (Shin Min-a), a former beauty turned overworked, overweight lawyer who hires celebrity trainer Kim Young-ho (So Ji-sub) to help her get healthy. The drama focuses on self-love, fitness, and building a romance as they overcome personal health struggles.

Shooting stars

Shooting Stars follows the busy staff of a talent management agency who work behind the scenes to handle the careers and scandals of celebrities. At the center of the story is the love-hate relationship between top actor Gong Tae-sung and hardworking PR team leader Oh Han-byul. As they constantly clash and bicker, their rivalry slowly turns into romance while showing the chaos of the entertainment industry.

Mr. Queen

is a South Korean historical fantasy-comedy about Jang Bong-hwan, a modern-day, arrogant Blue House chef whose soul becomes trapped in the body of Queen Kim So-yong (Shin Hye-sun) in the Joseon Dynasty. He must navigate dangerous palace politics, a sham marriage with the two-faced King Cheoljong (Kim Jung-hyun), and his new life as a woman while trying to return to the present.

So I married an Anti-fan

is a romantic comedy drama following Lee Geun-young, a magazine reporter who loses her job after a disastrous encounter with top Hallyu star Hoo Joon. Convinced he ruined her life, she becomes his top "anti-fan," eventually agreeing to star with him in a reality TV show about a celebrity living with their biggest hater.

My Roommate is a Gumiho

is a South Korean romantic fantasy drama about Shin Woo-yeo, a 999-year-old male nine-tailed fox (gumiho) trying to become human, who lives with Lee Dam, a college student who accidentally swallows his essential fox bead. They move in together to protect the bead, resulting in a romantic, comedic, and sometimes dangerous, relationship.

Dynamite Kiss

is a 14-episode South Korean romantic comedy on Netflix, starring Ahn Eun-jin as Go Da-rim and Jang Ki-yong as Gong Ji-hyeok. Da-rim, desperate for employment, disguises herself as a married mother to land a job at a baby products company, only to fall for her intense team leader, Ji-hyeok.

Pegasus Market

is a 2019 South Korean comedy-drama series based on a popular webtoon of the same name. The story follows the chaotic transformation of a struggling supermarket, Cheonrima Mart, a subsidiary of the large Daema Group, which serves as a dumping ground for demoted employees.

Gaus Electronics

is a 12-episode South Korean romantic comedy drama that follows the chaotic work and love lives of Marketing Team 3 at a multinational home appliance company. It centers on, Lee Sang-sik, an overly enthusiastic employee who causes trouble, and his hot-tempered manager, Cha Na-rae, as they navigate workplace mishaps.

The Fiery Priest

is a comedy-crime drama about Kim Hae-il (Kim Nam-gil), a hot-tempered Catholic priest with a past as an NIS agent. When an elderly priest is murdered and framed for sexual harassment, Hae-il teams up with a bumbling detective (Kim Sung-kyun) and a sharp prosecutor (Honey Lee) to take down corrupt officials and gangsters in the fictional Goudam city.

Because This Is My First Life

Because This Is My First Life follows a socially awkward homeowner and a struggling writer who enter a contract marriage for practical reasons. Living together slowly changes their views on love, relationships, and adulthood. The drama offers a warm and realistic look at careers, marriage, friendship, and finding happiness in modern life.

The Sound of Your Heart

is a South Korean sitcom based on the long-running webtoon of the same name by Jo Suk. It is a chaotic, over-the-top comedy that focuses on the ridiculous daily lives of a struggling webtoon artist, his equally eccentric family, and his girlfriend.

Dream

is a South Korean sports comedy-drama following Yoon Hong-dae (Park Seo-joon), a professional soccer player on disciplinary probation after an unexpected incident. He is forced to coach a ragtag national team of homeless individuals with no experience for the Homeless World Cup.

She Was Pretty

She Was Pretty follows Kim Hye-jin, a once-beautiful girl who grows up feeling insecure after losing her looks and confidence. When her handsome childhood friend returns and wants to meet her, she sends her prettier best friend in her place. As they begin working together at a fashion magazine, misunderstandings, romance, and self-worth become central to the story.

True Beauty

True Beauty tells the story of Lim Ju-kyung, a high school girl who masters makeup to hide her insecurities about her appearance. At school she becomes popular for her beauty, but she fears others discovering her bare face. As she gets caught between two very different boys, the drama explores love, confidence, and accepting oneself.
