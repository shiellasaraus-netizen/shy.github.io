Medical Dramas

Blood

Is a 20-episode fantasy medical thriller following Park Ji-sang (Ahn Jae-hyun), a brilliant hepatopancreatobiliary surgeon who is also a vampire. He works at a top cancer hospital, suppressing his thirst for blood to save terminally ill patients, while battling an evil director (Ji Jin-hee) researching the VBT-01 virus.

Doctor Romantic

Is a gripping medical drama following Boo Yong-joo (Han Suk-kyu), a triple-board certified "genius" surgeon who, after a traumatic incident, renames himself Kim Sa-bu and works at the rustic Doldam Hospital. He mentors young, struggling doctors—Kang Dong-joo and Yoon Seo-jeong—teaching them to prioritize patients over money and power.

Doctor Crush

Is a 2016 Korean medical romance drama following Yoo Hye-jung (Park Shin-hye), a rebellious, troubled teen who transforms into a dedicated neurosurgeon after being mentored by compassionate teacher Hong Ji-hong (Kim Rae-won). They reunite 13 years later at a hospital to navigate professional rivalry, romance, and past traumas.

Resident Playbook

Is a spin-off of Hospital Playlist that follows the turbulent lives, friendships, and professional growth of young obstetrician-gynecologists at the Jongno branch of Yulje Medical Center. It focuses on five first-year OB-GYN residents navigating burnout, steep learning curves, and patient care with a more introspective tone.

Hospital Playlist

Is a heartwarming, slice-of-life medical drama following five doctors in their 40s—Lee Ik-jun, Ahn Jeong-won, Kim Jun-wan, Yang Seok-hyeong, and Chae Song-hwa—who have been best friends since entering medical school in 1999. Working together at Yulje Medical Center, they navigate demanding careers, romantic relationships, and personal struggles, while resurrecting their college band to bond over music.

Doctor Slump

Is a romantic comedy following two brilliant doctors—former high school rivals Nam Ha-neul and Yeo Jeong-woo—who hit rock bottom simultaneously. After quitting their prestigious jobs due to extreme burnout, depression, and a malpractice conspiracy, they reunite, living in a rooftop room together and finding healing, love, and professional redemption in each other.

The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call

Is a high-stakes, 8-episode medical drama following Baek Kang-hyuk (Ju Ji-hoon), a brilliant, war-hardened trauma surgeon who takes over a struggling trauma center at Hankuk National University Hospital. He disrupts the status quo with aggressive, unconventional methods to save patients during the "golden hour," transforming his team from a nominal unit into a elite, life-saving squad, often navigating intense conflict with hospital administration.

Life

Is a character-driven medical thriller focusing on the intense power struggles within Sangkook University Hospital. The story pits passionate ER doctor Ye Jin-woo (Lee Dong-wook) against the new, profit-driven CEO Koo Seung-hyo (Cho Seung-woo), exploring the ethical conflict between patient care and corporate greed.

D-day

Is a 20-episode JTBC disaster-medical drama focusing on a passionate surgeon, Lee Hae-seong (Kim Young-kwang), and a team of medical professionals who struggle to save lives when a massive 6.5-magnitudeearthquake paralyzes Seoul. As infrastructure collapses, they fight against time, dwindling resources, and bureaucratic neglect in a ruined city.

Hyper Knife

Is a dark medical crime thriller on Disney+ following Jeong Se-ok (Park Eun-bin), a brilliant neurosurgeon turned underground "shadow" doctor after being disgraced by her former mentor, Dr. Choi Deok-hee (Sul Kyung-gu). When her mentor returns with a terminal brain tumor, it sparks a toxic, high-stakes battle of wits, vengeance, and complex moral dilemmas between the two geniuses.

Doctor Prisoner

Is a high-stakes South Korean thriller drama about Na Yi-je (Namkoong Min), a brilliant, kind-hearted ER doctor whose career is destroyed after a wealthy chaebol heir accuses him of malpractice. Seeking revenge, he secures a job as the chief of a prison medical clinic, exploiting the corrupt system to tear down the powerful elites responsible for his downfall.

Doctor Stranger

Is a 20-episode medical-spy thriller starring Lee Jong-suk as Park Hoon, a genius surgeon kidnapped and raised in North Korea. After fleeing to South Korea alone, he works at a top hospital while desperately searching for his lost love, Song Jae-hee (Jin Se-yeon). The series combines intense medical scenes with complex political conspiracies and romance.

Ghost Doctor

Is a fantasy medical drama about two doctors with opposite personalities and skills—genius but arrogant surgeon Cha Young-min (Rain) and wealthy but practically clumsy resident Go Seung-tak (Kim Bum)—who merge bodies and souls after a car accident leaves Young-min in a coma, forcing them to collaborate to save lives.

Live up to your name

Is a 16-episode fantasy-medical romance (available on Netflix and IMDb) following Heo Im, a skilled Joseon-era traditional acupuncturist, who travels 400 years into modern Seoul. He clashes with, then falls for, Choi Yeon-kyung, a modern cardiothoracic surgeon who dislikes oriental medicine.

Behind white tower

Is a critically acclaimed 20-episode medical-political drama that explores the intense power struggles, ambition, and malpractice within a university hospital. It focuses on the contrasting paths of a brilliant but cold surgeon, Jang Joon-hyuk, and his idealistic colleague, Choi Do-young.

Hospital Ship

Is a 40-episode romantic medical drama about young doctors providing care to rural island residents via a traveling ship. It follows talented but cold surgeon Song Eun-Jae (Ha Ji-won) who joins the ship after a personal tragedy, learning to connect with patients alongside warm-hearted internist Kwak Hyun (Kang Min-hyuk).

Doctor Cha

Is a 16-episode JTBC medical comedy-drama about Cha Jeong-suk, a housewife of 20 years who returns to her career as a first-year medical resident. After surviving a critical liver transplant, she decides to stop living for her family and pursue her dream, facing challenges at the same hospital where her unfaithful husband serves as chief surgeon.

Daily Dose of Sunshine

Is a 12-episode Netflix Korean drama following Jung Da-eun, a caring nurse transferred to a psychiatric ward, as she navigates patient care and mental health challenges. The series highlights themes of depression, trauma, and stigma, culminating in a message of hope, resilience, and the importance of seeking help.

Good Doctor

Is a 20-episode South Korean medical drama following Park Shi-on (Joo Won), a young man with autism and savant syndrome who overcomes prejudice to become a gifted pediatric surgeon. He possesses brilliant medical memory but struggles socially, facing skepticism from colleagues and hospital politics over six months to prove his capability.

Doctor John

Is a medical drama centering on Cha Yo-han (Ji Sung), a genius anesthesiologist who specializes in pain management, and Kang Shi-young (Lee Se-young), a warm-hearted resident. They investigate mysterious chronic pain cases, with the plot examining ethical debates on euthanasia and the right to die with dignity.
