Fantasy Dramas

Alchemy of Souls

Alchemy of Souls is set in a fictional country where powerful mages use “alchemy of souls,” a forbidden magic that allows souls to be switched between bodies. The story follows a powerful assassin trapped in a blind woman’s body who becomes the servant and teacher of a nobleman from a prestigious mage family. As their bond deepens, they face political conspiracies, identity struggles, and dangerous enemies while trying to control fate and recover lost powers.

Goblin (Guardian: The Lonely and Great God)

The Lonely and Great God is about a 900-year-old immortal goblin named Kim Shin who is cursed with eternal life and can only end it by finding his destined bride, the “Goblin’s Bride,” who can remove the sword in his chest. He unexpectedly becomes housemates with a cheerful high school girl, Ji Eun-tak, who claims to be his bride, leading to a mix of romance, humor, and tragedy as fate, past lives, and sacrifice intertwine with the Grim Reaper and other supernatural beings.

Hotel Del Luna

Hotel del Luna is a fantasy-romance drama about a mysterious hotel that only serves spirits before they move on to the afterlife. The hotel is managed by Jang Man-wol, a centuries-old woman bound by a past sin, and a new human manager who unexpectedly becomes involved in the world of ghosts. As they work together, hidden truths about Man-wol’s past are revealed, blending emotional healing, romance, and supernatural encounters.

The King: Eternal Monarch

The King: Eternal Monarch is about Emperor Lee Gon of the Kingdom of Corea, who discovers a parallel universe where modern-day South Korea exists. After a mysterious force opens a portal between the two worlds, he meets detective Jung Tae-eul, and together they try to stop a dangerous villain while navigating fate, identity, and love across parallel realities. The story blends romance, fantasy, and political intrigue as the characters fight to protect both worlds from collapsing.

Tale of the Nine Tailed

Tale of the Nine Tailed follows Lee Yeon, a powerful gumiho (nine-tailed fox) who once served as a guardian of the spirit world but now lives in the human world while searching for the reincarnation of his lost first love. As he works to eliminate supernatural threats, he becomes entangled with Nam Ji-ah, a fearless TV producer who looks exactly like the woman he has been waiting for. The story blends romance, action, and mythology as Lee Yeon faces dangerous spirits and uncovers secrets that connect their past lives across time.

The Witch

The Witch follows the story of a mysterious woman who has been labeled a “witch” due to strange misfortunes that seem to happen to anyone who gets close to her. Isolated and misunderstood, she lives a lonely life until a determined man begins to investigate the truth behind her rumored curse. As he digs deeper, he uncovers hidden trauma, emotional wounds, and a connection between fate and perception, leading to a story that challenges whether she is truly cursed or just a victim of fear and misunderstanding.

Moving

Moving follows teenagers who inherit superhuman abilities from their secret-agent parents while hiding their powers from the world. As mysterious assassinations begin targeting people with abilities, both parents and children are forced into dangerous missions that reveal hidden government operations and dark pasts. The series blends action, emotional family storytelling, and superhero elements centered on survival and identity.

My Demon

My Demon tells the story of a cold, wealthy heiress who becomes entangled with a powerful demon who temporarily loses his abilities after meeting her. When their fates become mysteriously linked, they enter a contract marriage that slowly turns into genuine love. The drama combines romance, fantasy, and comedy as both characters confront threats from the supernatural world and their growing feelings for each other.

The Judge from Hell

The Judge from Hell follows a cold-hearted judge who is actually a demon sent from hell to punish corrupt criminals by sending them to damnation. After taking over the body of a human judge, she begins delivering brutal justice while unexpectedly developing empathy through her interactions with a kind-hearted detective. The story mixes dark fantasy, crime, and moral conflict as she struggles between punishment and redemption.

Genie, Make a Wish

Genie, Make a Wish revolves around a mysterious genie who awakens after centuries and grants wishes to a modern-day woman who has lost her emotional spark. As she makes wishes that challenge fate and morality, both she and the genie begin to change through their interactions. The series blends magic, romance, and comedy while exploring themes of desire, consequence, and human emotion.

Doom at Your Service

Doom at Your Service tells the story of a woman diagnosed with a terminal illness who accidentally makes a contract with a supernatural being named Myul Mang, who brings doom to everything he touches. As they spend 100 days together, their relationship evolves into a deep and emotional romance that challenges the meaning of life, fate, and sacrifice. The drama combines melancholy fantasy with heartfelt love and existential themes.

Arthdal Chronicles

Arthdal Chronicles is set in a mythical ancient land called Arth, where different tribes struggle for power, survival, and the creation of civilization. It follows intertwined destinies of leaders, warriors, and prophets as they face war, betrayal, and prophecy that shape the birth of a new world order.

Bride of the Water God

Bride of the Water God follows Ha Baek, a water god who descends to the human world to find sacred stones needed to claim his throne. He meets psychiatrist So-ah, a woman destined to serve the gods, and their relationship develops from conflict into romance as divine responsibilities and human emotions collide.

W: Two Worlds

W: Two Worlds follows a surgeon who gets pulled into a webtoon world created by her father, where she meets the main character, Kang Chul. As she moves between reality and fiction, both worlds begin to overlap, creating dangerous consequences that blur the line between fate and free will.

A Korean Odyssey

A Korean Odyssey is a modern retelling of the classic Journey to the West, where a powerful immortal Son Oh-gong is bound by a magical contract with a woman who can see spirits. As demons, gods, and betrayals emerge, their complicated relationship becomes central to the fight between good and evil.

The Story of Park’s Marriage Contract

The Story of Park’s Marriage Contract follows Park Yeon-woo, a Joseon-era noblewoman who is suddenly transported to modern-day Seoul after being thrown into a well. There, she meets Kang Tae-ha, a cold chaebol heir who looks exactly like her deceased husband from the past. The two enter a contract marriage for convenience, but as Yeon-woo adapts to the modern world, hidden secrets about time travel, fate, and love across centuries begin to unfold.

My Love from the Star

My Love from the Star tells the story of Do Min-joon, an alien who has lived on Earth for centuries, and his unexpected romance with top actress Cheon Song-yi. As his time on Earth nears its end, he is forced to choose between returning to his planet or staying with the woman he loves.

Legend of the Blue Sea

Legend of the Blue Sea follows a mermaid who travels to the modern world and meets a genius con artist who resembles a man from her past life in the Joseon era. As their love story unfolds across different lifetimes, they must overcome fate, danger, and identity to stay together.

Memories of the Alhambra

Memories of the Alhambra centers on a CEO who becomes trapped in a mysterious augmented reality game set in Spain, where game glitches begin to affect real life. As he searches for the creator of the game, he becomes entangled in danger, betrayal, and a complex relationship with the developer’s sister.

While You Were Sleeping

While You Were Sleeping follows a young woman who has the ability to see future events in her dreams, usually involving tragic accidents or deaths. When she meets a prosecutor and a rookie detective who also begin experiencing similar dreams, the three work together to prevent the predicted incidents from happening. As they try to change fate, they become deeply connected through justice, romance, and the burden of knowing the future.
