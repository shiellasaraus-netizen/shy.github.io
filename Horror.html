Horror

If Wishes Could Kill

A supernatural thriller where wishes granted by a mysterious force come with deadly consequences. Each character believes they are getting what they want, but their desires slowly turn into tragic outcomes. The story explores how human greed and desperation can turn miracles into curses.

The Call

The Call is about two women from different timelines who become connected through a mysterious phone in an old house. At first, they help each other change their lives, but their actions start creating dangerous and irreversible consequences. The film builds intense suspense as past and present collide in deadly ways.

Revenant

Revenant follows a woman possessed by a mysterious spirit and a folklore expert who investigates strange deaths linked to cursed objects. As they dig deeper, they uncover hidden secrets about greed, sacrifice, and ancient rituals. The story blends horror and mystery as they fight an unseen evil.

The Guest

The Guest follows a psychic, a priest, and a detective who team up to fight a powerful demonic force. The spirit they hunt is responsible for brutal killings and long-hidden trauma. Their journey uncovers painful truths about possession, revenge, and faith.

All of Us Are Dead

All of Us Are Dead takes place in a high school where a zombie outbreak suddenly traps students inside. With no help from the outside, they must fight, escape, and survive while facing fear and betrayal. The series also shows emotional sacrifices and the loss of innocence during survival.

The Mimic

The Mimic is about a family that encounters a mysterious creature in the forest that can imitate human voices. This leads to disturbing disappearances and terrifying events that blur reality and illusion. The story builds a chilling atmosphere based on fear of the unknown.

Save Me

Save Me follows a girl trapped inside a dangerous cult disguised as a religious group. Her friends try to rescue her while facing psychological manipulation and violence. The drama exposes how blind faith can lead to control and abuse.

Stranger from Hell

Stranger from Hell follows a young man who moves into a strange boarding house filled with suspicious tenants. As unsettling events occur, he slowly loses his sanity and begins to question reality. The series focuses on psychological horror and human darkness.

Goedam

Goedam features short horror stories based on urban legends and supernatural folklore. Each episode presents a different terrifying scenario involving ghosts, curses, or unexplained events. The anthology creates quick but chilling horror experiences.

Exhuma

Exhuma follows a team of shamans and experts who are hired to investigate a cursed grave. Their discovery awakens dark forces tied to ancient rituals and buried secrets. The film mixes spiritual horror with mystery and cultural mythology.

Night Has Come

Night Has Come shows students forced into a deadly real-life mafia game during a school retreat. They must vote and eliminate each other to survive, leading to paranoia and betrayal. The story explores fear, trust, and survival instincts under pressure.

The Cursed

The Cursed follows a journalist and a mysterious girl who can curse people through voodoo-like abilities. A powerful corporation is secretly connected to the dark events happening around them. The series blends investigative journalism with supernatural horror.

Class of Lies

Class of Lies follows a lawyer who goes undercover as a teacher to investigate a murder in an elite school. As he gathers evidence, he uncovers corruption, lies, and hidden relationships among students. The story highlights justice and moral conflict.

Hellbound

Hellbound depicts a world where supernatural beings appear to condemn people to hell without warning. This causes fear, chaos, and the rise of extremist religious groups. The series explores belief, justice, and human morality.

Alive

Alive follows a young man trapped alone in his apartment during a zombie outbreak. He struggles to survive with limited resources until he discovers another survivor nearby. Together, they try to escape the infected city.

Dark Nuns

A horror story about nuns confronting powerful demonic forces within a religious setting. It combines exorcism themes with faith, sacrifice, and spiritual battles against evil. The story focuses on courage in the face of supernatural terror.

Divine Fury

The Divine Fury follows a martial artist who gains divine powers after a mysterious event. He teams up with an exorcist priest to fight demonic forces threatening humanity. The film blends action, faith, and supernatural battles.

Hidden Face

A psychological thriller centered on secrets, hidden identities, and deception in relationships. Characters struggle with truth and manipulation as hidden pasts are revealed. The story builds tension through mystery and emotional conflict.

Train to Busan

Train to Busan follows passengers trapped on a train during a sudden zombie outbreak in Korea. As chaos spreads, they fight to survive while facing moral choices and sacrifice. The film is known for its emotional storytelling and intense action.

Light Shop

Light Shop follows strangers who are mysteriously drawn to a strange light shop that exists between life and death. Each visitor carries emotional pain or unresolved past events. The series explores grief, memory, and supernatural connections between souls.
