Thriller

Vincenzo

Is a dark comedy-thriller about Vincenzo Cassano (Song Joong-ki), a Korean-Italian Mafia consigliere who returns to Seoul to recover hidden gold. He becomes embroiled in a fight against the corrupt Babel Group, ultimately using ruthless mafia tactics to achieve social justice alongside lawyer Hong Cha-young (Jeon Yeo-been) and the quirky tenants of Geumga Plaza.

My Name

Yoon Ji-Woo’s (Han So-Hee) father dies suddenly. She wants to desperately take revenge on whoever is responsible for her father's death. Yoon Ji-Woo works for drug crime group Dongcheonpa.Choi Mu-Jin (Park Hee-Soon) is the boss of the drug gang. With the help of Choi Mu-Jin and to uncover the reason for her father's death, Yoon Ji-Woo joins the police department and becomes a mole for the drug group. Yoon Ji-Woo is assigned to work in the drug investigation unit in the police department. Her partner there is Detective Jeon Pil-Do (Ahn Bo-Hyun).

Study Group

An action-comedy drama based on a Naver webtoon, starring Hwang Min-hyun as Yoon Ga-min, a student who looks like a model student but has zero talent for studying. He attends the notorious Yusung Technical High School, aiming to form a study group to get into college, but instead uses his elite fighting skills to protect his friends from school bullies and gangs.

Dear X

Is a dark psychological thriller following Baek Ah-jin (Kim Yoo-jung), a top actress with sociopathic tendencies who hides a cruel nature behind a beautiful facade. Driven by a traumatic, abusive past, she uses manipulation to rise to the top, but her world crumbles as her true self is exposed.

Taxi Driver

Is a 16-episode action-thriller Kdrama about Kim Do-gi, a former special forces officer turned vigilante driver for Rainbow Taxi Company. Along with a specialized team, he delivers "private revenge" to criminals who escaped justice, punishing them on behalf of victims. The drama explores ethical, legal, and social justice themes based on real-life crimes.

Gyeongseong Creature

Is a thriller following wealthy pawnshop owner Jang Tae-sang (Park Seo-joon) and tracker Yoon Chae-ok (Han So-hee). They investigate disappearances, uncovering a monstrous creature born of greedy human experimentation at Ongseong Hospital, inspired by Unit 731.

Death Game

Is a 2023 fantasy-thriller K-drama (8 episodes) based on a webtoon, following Choi Yee-jae (Seo In-guk), who attempts suicide after7 years of unemployment and financial ruin. Offended, Death (Park So-dam) forces him to experience 12 brutal, consecutive deaths in different bodies to learn the value of life.

The K2

Is a 2016 South Korean action thriller that follows Kim Je-ha, a former mercenary soldier known by the codename "K2." After being framed for the murder of his lover while serving in Iraq, Je-ha becomes a fugitive on a mission for revenge.

Flower of Evil

Is a 2020 thriller-romance K-drama about Cha Ji-won (Moon Chae-won), a homicide detective, who discovers her husband of 14 years, Baek Hee-sung (Lee Joon-gi), is living under a false identity and is suspected of being an accomplice to his serial killer father's crimes.

Mouse

Is an intense thriller following rookie officer Jung Ba-reum (Lee Seung-gi) and detective Go Moo-chi (Lee Hee-joon) as they hunt a psychopathic serial killer. Inspired by real events, the drama explores whether psychopaths can be detected in the womb, featuring complex twists, moral dilemmas, and heavy, dark suspense.

City Hunter

is an action-romance drama following Lee Yoon-sung (Lee Min-ho), a MIT-trained IT expert working in South Korea's Blue House. He seeks vengeance against five corrupt officials responsible for his father's death. As the "City Hunter," he exposes these figures while falling for bodyguard Kim Na-na.

The Negotiation

Is a high-stakes crime thriller starring Son Ye-jin as a calm police crisis negotiator, Ha Chae-yoon, who must confront Min Tae-gu (Hyun Bin), a cold-blooded weapons smuggler holding hostages. Over 21 hours, she negotiates via webcam to stop his deadly agenda and uncovers a massive conspiracy involving corrupt officials.

Happiness

Is an apocalyptic thriller set in a near-future high-rise apartment, where a failed drug causes a "mad person disease" (zombie-like) outbreak. Police officer Yoon Sae-bom and detective Jung Yi-hyun navigate a city-wide quarantine, battling both infected, bloodthirsty neighbors and human greed to survive within their sealed building.

Vagabond

Is an action-thriller following stuntman Cha Dal-gun (Lee Seung-gi), who investigates a mysterious plane crash that killed his nephew, uncovering a massive government corruption conspiracy. He teams up with NIS agent Go Hae-ri (Bae Suzy) to expose the truth, facing relentless danger and betrayal in a fast-paced story of vengeance and justice.

The Glory

Is a 16-episode Netflix thriller (split into two parts) following Moon Dong-eun (Song Hye-kyo), who, after suffering brutal, life-altering high school bullying, dedicates her adult life to an elaborate, meticulous revenge scheme against her perpetrators and the bystanders who ignored her pain.

The Worst of Evil

The story centers on Ji Chang-wook as Park Jun-mo, a dedicated rural police officer who is often belittled by his in-laws for his low rank. Seeking a promotion to prove his worth, he accepts a dangerous mission to infiltrate the Gangnam Union, a powerful crime organization that controls the drug trade between South Korea, China, and Japan.

Memorist

Is a 16-episode, fast-paced thriller drama based on a popular webtoon, following Dong Baek (Yoo Seung-ho), a detective with the supernatural ability to read memories by touching people. He partners with a brilliant profiler, Han Sun-mi (Lee Se-young), to hunt down a brutal serial killer, uncovering hidden, personal past traumas in the process.

Friendly Rivalry

Is a 16-episode Netflix teen thriller that follows Woo Seul-gi (Chung Su-bin), an orphanage-raised transfer student who enters an elite high school for the top 1%, only to encounter the school's "queen," Yoo Jae-yi (Hyeri). They develop a tense, toxic, and obsessed friendship amidst intense competition, drug use, and a mystery surrounding Seul-gi’s father's death.

Healer

Is an action-romance thriller about an illegal "night courier" codenamed Healer (Ji Chang-wook) who teams up with a tabloid reporter (Park Min-young) and a famous journalist (Yoo Ji-tae) to uncover the truth behind a 1992 incident involving five friends, fighting against corrupt media tycoons while falling in love.

Big Mouth

Is a crime-thriller drama about Park Chang-ho (Lee Jong-suk), an incompetent lawyer with a 10%-win rate nicknamed "Big Mouth". After being framed for a murder case and mistaken for a genius conman named "Big Mouse," he is imprisoned and forced to become a real criminal to survive and expose a massive, upper-class conspiracy.
